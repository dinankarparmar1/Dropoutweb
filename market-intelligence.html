Sector Analysis

Which sectors are leading, which are lagging

The index rarely moves in a straight line — usually one or two sectors are doing the heavy lifting while others drag. Comparing sector bellwethers against the benchmark shows whether a move is broad-based or concentrated in a handful of stocks.

Live pricing from TradingView. Sector Leaders uses one bellwether stock per sector as a proxy for that sector's direction, not a full sectoral index.

Market Breadth

Is strength broad, or a handful of stocks?

An index can rise even while more stocks are falling than rising — that's the difference between a healthy move and a fragile one. Breadth measures participation: advances vs declines, new highs vs new lows, and how many stocks trade above their long-term average.

Institutional Activity

Where the big money is flowing

Foreign (FII) and domestic (DII) institutions move enough capital to shift the market on their own. Their net daily buying or selling, published after market close, is one of the most closely watched numbers in Indian markets.

Insider Trading

Promoter & insider transactions

Under SEBI's Prohibition of Insider Trading (PIT) rules, promoters, directors and designated employees must disclose their own trades in company stock. These filings don't predict price moves, but sustained buying or selling by the people who know a company best is worth watching.

Good to Know

Questions about this page

Is this data live?

Index prices, sector leaders and the heatmaps (Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, and the sector-by-sector map) update live from TradingView. Institutional activity, insider trading, and the Advances vs Declines breadth stat are pulled automatically from NSE's public reports once a day after market close. New 52-Week Highs vs Lows and % above 200-DMA aren't wired up yet — those two cards say so directly rather than showing a placeholder number.

Where can I check FII/DII and insider trading data myself right now?

NSE and BSE both publish this daily, and SEBI publishes insider trading disclosures under its PIT regulations. Direct links are in the Institutional Activity and Insider Trading sections above.

Is any of this investment advice?
