Your AI, Given a Presence

A holographic orb you can spin and zoom with your bare hands — no mouse, no controller, just a webcam.

What am I looking at?

This is the same Ivaan that powers chat, document analysis, and your AI Coach across the site — just given a visual form. Nothing here is a separate AI; it's a rendering of the same assistant, running entirely in your browser. Your camera feed never leaves your device — hand positions are processed locally and never uploaded anywhere.
