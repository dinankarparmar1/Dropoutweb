My Story

From a small town to the national stage.

I grew up in a small town, in a family with humble means and big hopes. Long before I understood charts, I was curious about money: how it grows, how it's protected, and how it can give an ordinary family real freedom.

Everyone around me had a safer plan. I was expected to become a lawyer, and I enrolled in law school. But at 21, I made the hardest decision of my life: I walked away from law to follow finance. In a small town, that choice comes with questions, doubts and a lot of silence.

What carried me through was one person who never stopped believing: my father, Surendra Singh Parmar. When others doubted, he stood behind me. Guided by my mentor Akash Joshi and backed by friends who stayed through every moment of self-doubt, I studied, practised and kept going until I earned my NISM certification.

That is where Dropout Traders comes from: hard work, dedication and discipline. Today my mission is simple. I want to make sure young people from towns like mine get the financial education I had to fight for.
