What Is IPO GMP?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is the premium at which IPO shares are informally traded before they list on the stock exchange. If an IPO has an issue price of ₹100 and a GMP of ₹20, the grey market expects it to list around ₹120.

The grey market is unofficial and unregulated. SEBI, NSE and BSE do not publish or endorse GMP, and there is no guarantee that a stock will list at the price GMP suggests.

How to Read GMP

  • Estimated listing price = Issue price + GMP
  • GMP % = GMP ÷ Issue price × 100
  • A negative GMP means the grey market expects a listing below the issue price.
  • GMP can swing sharply in the last few days before listing, so always look at the latest update time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GMP legal and official?

GMP comes from informal grey-market trading that is not regulated by SEBI or the exchanges. It is widely reported, but it is not an official price and has no legal backing.

Does a high GMP guarantee listing gains?

No. Many IPOs with a high GMP have listed far lower than expected, and some have listed at a loss. Treat GMP as one rough signal, not a prediction.

Where does this data come from?

IPO details — company name, price band, lot size, issue size, dates and live subscription — are taken automatically from NSE India's and BSE India's public IPO pages a few times every trading day. The listing date is an estimate (T+3 working days after the issue closes) unless NSE has announced it. GMP is not published by any exchange; our team collects it from market sources and updates it by hand, so it may be delayed or differ from other websites.

Should I apply for an IPO based on GMP?

We do not give advice on whether to apply for any IPO. Read the company's RHP, understand the business and its valuation, and consult a SEBI-registered adviser if you need advice.
